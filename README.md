# marketing-channel-analysis
Analysis of marketing campaign performance across different channels to understand lead generation, conversions, and customer acquisition efficiency.

## Business Context
A company runs multiple digital marketing campaigns across different acquisition channels. The marketing team wants to understand which channels are driving the best performance in terms of leads, conversions, and customer acquisition.

## Objective
Analyze marketing campaign data to evaluate channel performance in terms of lead generation, conversion rates, and customer acquisition efficiency.

## Key Questions
1. Which channels generate the most leads?
2. Which channels have the highest conversion rate?
3. Which campaigns perform best?
4. Which channels bring higher-quality customers?
5. How does performance vary over time?
